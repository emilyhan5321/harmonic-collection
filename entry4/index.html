Japan, Kyoto 🛬 🇯🇵 🍡

This was my first trip to Japan and traveling alone. Japan is a country I always wanted to visit but never to the chance to. First stop, I landed at Kansai Airport at Osaka, Japan. From here, I traveled around 4 other cities.

The first city I visited was Kyoto, Japan.

This city had the mood that I always imagined Japan would be like. Also, Kyoto, as it is a very traditional city, it had various nature and temples which I enjoyed walking through.

When I entered the hotel, I knew I had to go to the best spaces in the building. So, I went to the private onsen (hot spring).
The next morning, I had the most well decorated breakfast. I needed this meal because this day, I walked and walked and walked.

Finally, I visited the bamboo forest in Arashiyama, Kyoto. There was a huge line of traverlers from all over the globe. I think it was a good choice to travel alone.
